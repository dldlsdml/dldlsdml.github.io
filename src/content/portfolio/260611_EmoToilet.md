## Project Overview: Emo Toilet

* **Course & Scope:** Developed as part of the **GAMES3010** course, focusing on designing and delivering a fully playable single-level 3D game prototype meeting strict academic requirements.
* **Genre / Platform:** 3D Puzzle / Windows (PC Download)
* **Team Size & Environment:** Cross-cultural Team (International & Korean members, 5 members: G, Camila, Seoyool, Chaean, Seoyun) / Unreal Engine, Perforce, Maya
* **Concept & Story:** A unique, humorous 3D puzzle game featuring an emo-themed fish that travels down the toilet pipes to escape to the ocean, only to be caught by a bird at the very end.

---

## 1. Cross-Cultural Collaboration & Communication
* **Managing a Multilingual / Multi-background Team:** Collaborated effectively within a diverse team consisting of both international and Korean members.
* **Bridge & Alignment:** Overcame communication barriers by establishing clear documentation, structured guidelines, and centralized alignment to ensure all team members stayed synchronized on milestones and deliverables.

---

## 2. Structured Documentation & Pipeline Management
* **Comprehensive Map Planning:** Formulated and documented a detailed **Map Plan** prior to production, mapping out spatial flows, obstacle placements, and asset integration steps to prevent bottlenecks.
* **Version Control (Perforce):** Implemented Perforce for reliable asset and blueprint version management across the team.
* **Standardized Conventions:** Enforced strict project hygiene, including standardized naming conventions (`BP_Name`, `MM_Name`, `MI_Name`, `ANI_Name`) and a uniform stylized material workflow.

---

## 3. Roles & Contributions
* **Asset & System Integration:** Managed low-poly modeling textures and stylized master materials according to the emo-punk aesthetic guidelines (Black, Grey, White, Red color scheme).
* **Blueprint & Polish:** Implemented core interactive logic and obstacle movements to ensure smooth gameplay pacing across the level.
